# Ratings HellGate (theme 26, Demon Shiba)

Self-review of the 30 models (`build_HellGate.py`, previews and `stage_HellGate.png` in `out/HellGate/`), score 1-10 on
silhouette, theme clarity, proportion/attachment, low-poly consistency and wow. Everything below 8 was remade. R1 = first build,
R2 = after the first round of changes, Final = what ships. All models: one mesh, flat vertex colours, no Neon, 600 to 2076 triangles
(Ground is the largest at 2076: it covers the whole 180 x 140 plot; the rest stay under 1700).
