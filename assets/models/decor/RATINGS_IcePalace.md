# Ratings: Decor IcePalace (bay 20, Frost Shiba)

Scores 1-10 (mean of silhouette, theme clarity, proportion/attachment, low-poly consistency, wow). Below 8 was remade; Final is the shipped score.
Every preview and `stage_IcePalace.png` in `out/IcePalace/` was looked at. Build: `blender --background --factory-startup --python build_IcePalace.py -- <absolute outdir> [PartId ...]`.
25 models (12 first batch, 13 second batch), 1 mesh each, flat vertex colours, no Neon, all fitted to the union box of their blueprint pieces.
