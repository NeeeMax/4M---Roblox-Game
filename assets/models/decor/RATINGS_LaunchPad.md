# LaunchPad decor ratings

Score 1-10 per round (silhouette at distance, theme clarity, proportion/attachment of details, low-poly consistency, wow).
Anything below 8 was remade (up to 4 rounds). Triangles are the final counts (limit 1500, max 4 meshes; all models are 1 mesh).
All models are built by `build_LaunchPad.py` from `blueprint_LaunchPad.json` without the part Yaw; every model is fitted to the union box of its blueprint pieces (fit scales 0.94 to 1.01).
