# Ratings MechaHangar (theme of bay 23, Mecha Shiba)

Self-review of the 30 models, 1-10 per part (silhouette, theme clarity, proportion/attachment, low-poly consistency, wow), judged on
`out/MechaHangar/preview_<Part>.png` (3/4 view with a 7-stud reference Shiba) and `stage_MechaHangar.png` (3/4 + top view of the whole stage).
Below 8 was remade (2 rounds). R1 = first render, R2 = after the remake, Final = last render. Triangle maximum: 1476 (Hangar), all parts
are 1 mesh, flat vertex colours, one palette (steel blues, panel grey, hazard yellow/black, orange, red, holo cyan), no Neon.
