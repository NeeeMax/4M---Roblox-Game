# Ratings Beanstalk (theme 27, Giant Shiba, Beanstalk and Stone Circle)

Self-review of the 30 models (`build_Beanstalk.py`, previews and `stage_Beanstalk.png` in `out/Beanstalk/`), score 1-10 on
silhouette, theme clarity, proportion/attachment, low-poly consistency and wow. Everything below 8 was remade. R1 = first build,
Final = what ships. All models: one mesh, flat vertex colours, no Neon, 444 to 1904 triangles (the previews carry a 16 stud Shiba).

Notes
- Previews are rendered in the exported (x-mirrored) frame, so they match what Studio shows; text reads correctly from +z.
- Fit scales are between 0.93 and 1.09 in every axis (models are built to the union boxes of the blueprint pieces).
- Largest models: Roots 1904, Stem 1708, GoldHoard 1640 triangles (a bit above the 1500 target, kept for the look).
- Tufts and small details that would stick out of a part's blueprint box are skipped automatically (BOX in the build script).
- Giant things are scaled for the 16 stud Shiba: stool 17 tall, table 14 tall (top), boots 15.7, club 39, obelisk 29.6, stalk 74 + nest 90.
